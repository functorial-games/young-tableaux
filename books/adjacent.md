# Adjacent books and long references

These are not all "Young tableaux books." They are included because the same structures reappear nearby and may be worth cross-linking later.

## Coxeter / type A

- **Anders Björner, Francesco Brenti — _Combinatorics of Coxeter Groups_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-27596-7
  - Type A is S_n; reduced words, Bruhat order and parabolic structure provide a second language for symmetric-group combinatorics.

- **Drew Armstrong — _Generalized Noncrossing Partitions and Combinatorics of Coxeter Groups_**
  - AMS: https://pubs.ams.org/ebooks/memo/0949/
  - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611106
  - Especially relevant for type-A noncrossing partitions and the larger Coxeter-family viewpoint.

## Crystals / quantum groups

- **Daniel Bump and Anne Schilling — _Crystal Bases: Representations and Combinatorics_**
  - Author research page: https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~anne/research.html
  - Tableaux become explicit models for crystal graphs and highest-weight representations.

- **Susumu Ariki — _Representations of Quantum Algebras and Combinatorics of Young Tableaux_**
  - AMS University Lecture Series 26: https://bookstore.ams.org/view?ProductCode=ULECT%2F26
  - Directly extends the tableaux story toward quantum algebras / Hecke-type representation theory.

- **_k-Schur Functions and Affine Schubert Calculus_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4939-0682-6
  - Affine extension of symmetric-function/Schubert/tableau combinatorics.

## Schubert / degeneracy-locus side

- **Laurent Manivel — _Symmetric Functions, Schubert Polynomials and Degeneracy Loci_**
  - Société Mathématique de France: https://smf.emath.fr/publications/fonctions-symetriques-polynomes-de-schubert-et-lieux-de-degenerescence-0
  - Makes the symmetric-function-to-geometry bridge explicit.

## General representation theory

- **William Fulton, Joe Harris — _Representation Theory: A First Course_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4612-0979-9

- **Pavel Etingof et al. — _Introduction to Representation Theory_**
  - AMS: https://bookstore.ams.org/stml-59
  - Author materials: https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/books.html

These broader texts are useful when a combinatorial rule is easy to compute but its representation-theoretic meaning is not yet clear.
