# Further reading

Additional books and anthologies that are close enough to the tableaux/symmetric-group neighborhood to keep indexed here.

- **Joseph P. S. Kung (ed.) — _Young Tableaux in Combinatorics, Invariant Theory, and Algebra: An Anthology of Recent Work_**
  - Elsevier: https://shop.elsevier.com/books/young-tableaux-in-combinatorics-invariant-theory-and-algebra/kung/978-0-12-428780-8
  - Anthology including reverse plane partitions, hook formulas, shifted tableaux, Schensted constructions, Littlewood–Richardson material, random tableaux and invariant theory.

- **Amritanshu Prasad — _Representation Theory: A Combinatorial Viewpoint_**
  - Cambridge: https://www.cambridge.org/us/universitypress/subjects/mathematics/algebra/representation-theory-combinatorial-viewpoint
  - Covers symmetric groups, symmetric functions, polynomial GL representations, RSK and Schur–Weyl duality from a combinatorial viewpoint.

- **Alexander Kleshchev — _Linear and Projective Representations of Symmetric Groups_**
  - Cambridge: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/linear-and-projective-representations-of-symmetric-groups/D8969B094B84784DC392EC38CCAD2100
  - A route into modular/projective representation theory and the modern symmetric-group/Hecke-algebra story.

- **James A. Green, Manfred Schocker, Karin Erdmann — _Polynomial Representations of GL_n: with an Appendix on Schensted Correspondence and Littelmann Paths_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-46944-3
  - Useful GL_n/Schur-algebra counterpart to the symmetric-group story, with explicit Schensted/Littelmann material.

- **Roe Goodman, Nolan R. Wallach — _Symmetry, Representations, and Invariants_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-79852-3
  - Broad representation/invariant theory; includes symmetric groups and Young tableaux in a wider Lie/algebraic-groups setting.

- **Susumu Ariki — _Representations of Quantum Algebras and Combinatorics of Young Tableaux_**
  - AMS: https://bookstore.ams.org/view?ProductCode=ULECT%2F26
  - Direct path from tableaux to quantum algebras, Hecke algebras and related representation theory.

- **Laurent Manivel — _Symmetric Functions, Schubert Polynomials and Degeneracy Loci_**
  - SMF: https://smf.emath.fr/publications/fonctions-symetriques-polynomes-de-schubert-et-lieux-de-degenerescence-0
  - Strong geometry/symmetric-functions bridge.

- **_k-Schur Functions and Affine Schubert Calculus_**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4939-0682-6
  - Useful when the ordinary tableaux/Schur story is extended toward affine type A.
