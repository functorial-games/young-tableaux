# Lecture notes and course material

Public lecture notes are useful here because they often expose the sequence of definitions and examples more directly than a monograph.

## Symmetric functions / algebraic combinatorics

- **Sergey Fomin — Math 665: Symmetric Functions (University of Michigan)**
  - https://dept.math.lsa.umich.edu/~fomin/665f15.html
  - Reading list explicitly points to Stanley, Fulton, Macdonald and Sagan; good hub for cross-references.

- **Alistair Savage — Symmetric Functions**
  - https://alistairsavage.ca/symfunc/notes/Savage-SymmetricFunctions.pdf
  - Modern lecture notes centered on symmetric functions.

- **Steven Sam — Math 740: Symmetric Functions**
  - https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~ssam/old/17S-740/notes.pdf
  - Graduate notes with an algebraic-combinatorics orientation.

- **Alex Postnikov — 18.217 Combinatorial Theory (MIT, 2020)**
  - https://math.mit.edu/~apost/courses/18.217_2020/

- **Alex Postnikov — 18.217 Combinatorial Theory (MIT, 2022)**
  - https://math.mit.edu/~apost/courses/18.217_2022/

- **Alex Postnikov — 18.217 Combinatorial Theory (MIT, 2023)**
  - https://math.mit.edu/~apost/courses/18.217_2023/

- **Alex Postnikov — 18.212 Algebraic Combinatorics (MIT, 2024)**
  - https://math.mit.edu/~apost/courses/18.212_2024/

- **Alex Postnikov — 18.315 Combinatorial Theory (MIT, 2006)**
  - https://math.mit.edu/~apost/courses/18.315-2006/

- **Igor Pak course / UCLA algebraic-combinatorics notes**
  - https://www.math.ucla.edu/~pak/courses/206-Winter-2019/206B-Algebraic-Combinatorics-Raban-course-notes.pdf

## Symmetric-group representation theory

- **Andrew Snowden course, notes by Aleksander Horawa — Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups**
  - https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/horawa/math_711.pdf
  - Explicitly uses James and Fulton–Harris as core references; good bridge between classical and more modern viewpoints.

- **Representation Theory of the Symmetric Group — Royal Holloway notes**
  - https://www.ma.rhul.ac.uk/~uvah099/Maths/Sym/SymGroup2014.pdf

- **Liam Tomczak / Michigan course notes — Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups**
  - https://math.berkeley.edu/~ltomczak/notes/Mich2022/RepSn_Notes.pdf

- **David Speyer — Introduction to Representation Theory**
  - https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/speyer/wp-content/uploads/sites/1332/2024/08/CourseNotes.pdf
  - Broader than S_n but useful for keeping the combinatorial objects tied to actual representation theory.

- **Cornell representation-theory notes**
  - https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~dmehrle/notes/partiii/reptheory_partiii_notes.pdf

## RSK / tableaux tutorials

- **Sage thematic tutorial — Young's lattice and the RSK algorithm**
  - https://sporadic.stanford.edu/thematic_tutorials/algebraic_combinatorics/rsk.html
  - A computationally oriented narrative connecting Young's lattice and RSK.

- **Classical crystals / tableaux tutorial**
  - https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/thematic_tutorials/lie/crystals.html
  - Connects semistandard tableaux to GL_n representations, Schur functions, Schur–Weyl duality and crystals.
