# Papers and surveys

A working map of papers that define or illuminate the main structures around Young tableaux. Prefer journal, author, institutional, or arXiv links.

## Robinson–Schensted–Knuth and subsequences

- **G. de B. Robinson (1938), "On the Representations of the Symmetric Group."**
  - Stable scan used in a University of Washington course: https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/561.fall.2019/articles/Robinson.1938.pdf
  - Historical starting point for the Robinson correspondence.

- **C. Schensted (1961), "Longest Increasing and Decreasing Subsequences."**
  - Canadian Journal of Mathematics: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-journal-of-mathematics/article/longest-increasing-and-decreasing-subsequences/B5098D9BC8B226C575402B971852C05E
  - Connects insertion/tableaux shape to longest increasing and decreasing subsequences.

- **Donald E. Knuth (1970), "Permutations, Matrices, and Generalized Young Tableaux."**
  - Pacific Journal of Mathematics PDF: https://msp.org/pjm/1970/34-3/pjm-v34-n3-p09-p.pdf
  - Generalizes the correspondence from permutations to matrices/words: the K in RSK.

- **Curtis Greene (1974), "An Extension of Schensted's Theorem."**
  - Haverford repository: https://scholarship.haverford.edu/mathematics_facpubs/172/
  - Refines the LIS/LDS information in tableau shape via unions of increasing/decreasing subsequences.

- **Dominique Foata and Marcel-Paul Schützenberger / later expository treatment of the algorithms**
  - Electronic Journal of Combinatorics: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v3i2r15/pdf/
  - Useful algorithmic/expository route through Robinson–Schensted and Schützenberger operations.

## Hook lengths and enumeration

- **J. S. Frame, G. de B. Robinson, R. M. Thrall (1954), "The Hook Graphs of the Symmetric Group."**
  - Canadian Journal of Mathematics: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-journal-of-mathematics/article/hook-graphs-of-the-symmetric-group/B1533D44F5B1DBF6B6903C79AFFDCFEE
  - Classical source for the hook-length formula.

- **Jean-Christophe Novelli, Igor Pak, Alexander V. Stoyanovskii, "A direct bijective proof of the hook-length formula."**
  - DMTCS article: https://dmtcs.episciences.org/en/articles/239
  - Author PDF: https://www.math.ucla.edu/~pak/papers/bij.pdf
  - Particularly relevant if a future interaction exposes the hook-length formula as a local combinatorial process rather than merely a computed number.

- **Bruce Sagan, "The Ubiquitous Young Tableau."**
  - Author PDF: https://users.math.msu.edu/users/bsagan/papers/old/uyt.pdf
  - Survey connecting tableaux to enumeration, representation theory, symmetric functions and RSK.

## Jeu de taquin, Littlewood–Richardson, plactic structure

- **Marc A. A. van Leeuwen, "The Littlewood-Richardson rule, and related combinatorics."**
  - Author/university PDF: http://www-math.univ-poitiers.fr/~maavl/pdf/lrr.pdf
  - Detailed combinatorial account of LR-type constructions.

- **Lascoux–Schützenberger, "Schubert polynomials and the Littlewood-Richardson rule."**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00398147
  - Bridge from tableaux/symmetric functions toward Schubert-polynomial geometry.

- **Schützenberger's jeu de taquin — expository notes**
  - https://mps2016.labri.fr/archives/feray.pdf

- **Plactic-monoid chapter / notes**
  - University of Minnesota-hosted chapter: https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~reiner/REU/Lothaire_plactic_monoid_chapter.pdf
  - Useful when tracing the equivalence relation on words induced by Knuth relations and insertion tableaux.

## Growth diagrams, Young lattice, differential posets

- **Richard P. Stanley (1988), "Differential Posets."**
  - AMS/JAMS: https://www.ams.org/journals/jams/1988-01-04/S0894-0347-1988-0941434-9/
  - Young's lattice is the central example; gives an operator/poset viewpoint on branching and tableau enumeration.

- **Sergey Fomin, "Duality of Graded Graphs."**
  - EMIS full text: https://emis.muni.cz/journals/JACO/Volume3_4/bx4c3m5xlcdmtgu3.fulltext.pdf
  - General framework behind growth diagrams and correspondences.

- **Sergey Fomin, "Schensted Algorithms for Dual Graded Graphs."**
  - EMIS full text: https://emis.muni.cz/journals/JACO/Volume4_1/j3330612tm0w1m47.fulltext.pdf
  - Makes local growth rules explicit; unusually relevant to eventual interactive representations because global correspondences arise from local moves.

- **Krattenthaler, "RSK correspondence, jeu de taquin, and growth diagrams."**
  - https://mps2016.labri.fr/archives/krattenthaler.pdf
  - Expository bridge among several equivalent algorithmic pictures.

## Symmetric-group representation theory

- **Andrei Okounkov and Anatoly Vershik (1996), "A New Approach to Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups."**
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02433451
  - arXiv version: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503040
  - Branching/Gelfand–Tsetlin/Jucys–Murphy viewpoint; especially natural when thinking about paths in the Young graph.

- **Vershik–Okounkov related inductive exposition**
  - Russian Mathematical Surveys / IOP: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1070/RM1996v051n02ABEH002897

## Random tableaux, Plancherel measure, asymptotic shape

- **B. F. Logan and L. A. Shepp (1977), "A Variational Problem for Random Young Tableaux."**
  - Advances in Mathematics: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0001870877900305
  - One of the classic limit-shape papers.

- **Jinho Baik, Percy Deift, Kurt Johansson (1999), "On the Distribution of the Length of the Longest Increasing Subsequence of Random Permutations."**
  - AMS/JAMS: https://pubs.ams.org/JAMS/1999-12-04/S0894-0347-99-00307-0/
  - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9810105
  - RSK connects LIS to random Young diagrams and random-matrix asymptotics.

- **Borodin–Okounkov–Olshanski, "Asymptotics of Plancherel Measures for Symmetric Groups."**
  - AMS/JAMS: https://www.ams.org/journals/jams/2000-13-03/S0894-0347-00-00337-4/
  - Further asymptotic/probabilistic structure on Young diagrams.

- **Ivanov–Olshanski / Kerov central-limit material**
  - arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0304010
  - Useful later if random-diagram behavior becomes a separate experiment.

## Schubert / geometry side

- **Mark Haiman, expository Schubert-polynomial material**
  - https://math.berkeley.edu/~mhaiman/ftp/schubert/schubert.pdf

- **Sara Billey, "Lectures on Schubert Varieties."**
  - https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/schubert.notes/notes.pdf

- **Schubert calculus on Grassmannians — lecture notes**
  - https://people.math.osu.edu/anderson.2804/ecag/pre_ch09.pdf
  - Useful for seeing Schur functions and Littlewood–Richardson coefficients as geometry rather than isolated combinatorial formulas.
